# Gamification Project: Solving Challenges in Remote Work

Based on the assignment requirements, this document outlines real
business problems associated with remote work, supporting research, and
a proposed gamification solution.

## 1. The problem

Remote employees have fewer informal interactions, which can reduce team
engagement and knowledge sharing. A gamified approach could encourage
social interaction and collaboration through cooperative quests, team
challenges and shared progress. This topic has a clear relationship to
cooperative game mechanics and can be tested with classmates.

## 2. Scientific research: Evidence of real problems

### Social isolation and reduced team connection

#### Study 1: Gallup (2026)

**Source:** *State of the Global Workplace: 2026 Report*

Gallup investigated employees' workplace experiences, including
loneliness and engagement.

  Working Arrangement                  Employees Experiencing Daily Loneliness
  ---------------------------------- -----------------------------------------
  Exclusively remote                                                       24%
  Hybrid                                                                   24%
  On-site, remote-capable jobs                                             18%
  On-site, non-remote-capable jobs                                         21%

The survey used data collected in 2025 and published in April 2026.

**Interpretation:** Employees working remotely or in hybrid arrangements
reported loneliness more frequently than employees working on-site in
remote-capable jobs. These figures describe associations; they do not
establish that remote work causes loneliness for every employee.

**Relevance:** A gamified intervention could encourage meaningful
interactions between employees, potentially strengthening relationships
and reducing feelings of isolation.

Source: [Gallup -- State of the Global Workplace
2026](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/349484/state-of-the-global-workplace.aspx)

#### Study 2: Cho et al. (2025)

**Title:** *How remote workers manage loneliness and performance:
Virtual social interaction as an alternative resource management
strategy*

Published in *Human Resource Development Quarterly*.

The researchers conducted a five-day diary study involving 170
remote-working employees and 620 daily observations.

**Key findings:** - Reduced personal social interactions were associated
with increased feelings of loneliness. - Loneliness was associated with
reduced psychological resources. - Reduced resources were associated
with lower task performance and helping behavior. - Virtual social
interaction during working hours could help replenish employees'
resources and protect performance.

The research was conducted during the COVID-19 pandemic, which limits
how confidently the findings can be generalized to all remote workplaces
today.

**Gamification opportunity:** Create voluntary activities that encourage
employees to interact and help each other, such as collaborative quests,
knowledge-sharing challenges, and virtual team missions.

Source: [Search for the study by
title](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=How+remote+workers+manage+loneliness+and+performance)

#### Study 3: Informal communication and knowledge sharing (2026)

**Title:** *Beyond Where We Work: Daily Informal Communication,
Knowledge Sharing, and Commitment in Hybrid Teams*

Published in *Administrative Sciences*.

This research used a two-week diary study to investigate communication
and commitment in hybrid teams.

**Key findings:** - On remote working days, participants reported less
informal communication with coworkers. - They also reported less
knowledge sharing. - Lower informal communication and knowledge sharing
were associated with lower same-day team commitment.

**Relevance:** The project could focus on encouraging informal
communication and knowledge exchange through gamification.

Source: [Search for the study by
title](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Beyond+Where+We+Work+Daily+Informal+Communication+Knowledge+Sharing+and+Commitment+in+Hybrid+Teams)

types. People can display combinations of these motivations rather than
belonging exclusively to one type.

For RemoteConnect, four types are particularly relevant:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Player Type             Motivation              Suitable Game Mechanic
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Socialiser              Connecting with         Team missions and
                          colleagues              collaborative
                                                  activities

  Achiever                Personal improvement    Progress tracking and

  Philanthropist          Helping colleagues and  Knowledge-sharing
                          contributing to the     missions and

  Free Spirit             Independence and choice Optional quests and
                                                  customizable activities

**Example:** A Socialiser might enjoy a virtual coffee chat, while a

## 5. Game mechanics and dynamics

### Mechanic 1: Cooperative quests

Employees receive optional weekly activities.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Quest                   Activity                Reward
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Coffee Connection       Have a 10-minute        Connection badge
                          virtual coffee with a   
                          colleague               

  Knowledge Hero          Share a useful          Knowledge badge
                          work-related tip        

  Team Challenge          Solve a small challenge Team progress
                          together                

  Helping Hand            Help a colleague with a Appreciation badge
                          work-related problem    

  Meet Someone New        Exchange ideas with a   Explorer badge
                          colleague from another  
                          department              
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

These rewards are illustrative design choices, not validated incentive
levels.

### Mechanic 2: Shared progress

Instead of competing against colleagues, employees contribute toward a
common team goal.

Example: **Complete 20 voluntary collaborative activities as a team this
month.**

The platform visually displays progress. This encourages cooperation
rather than individual competition.

### Mechanic 3: Recognition and badges

Employees can recognize colleagues who voluntarily help others.

Example achievements: - Team Connector - Knowledge Champion -
Collaboration Hero

The badges acknowledge helpful behavior without making it part of
employee performance evaluations.

### Mechanic 4: Freedom of choice

Employees select the activities they enjoy. They can also decline
participation without consequences.

This is important because forced social interactions may reduce
motivation rather than increase it.

## 6. Scientific framework: Self-Determination Theory

The theoretical justification can be based on **Self-Determination
Theory (SDT)**, which identifies three important psychological needs:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Psychological Need      Meaning                 RemoteConnect
                                                  Application
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Autonomy                Feeling free to make    Employees choose their
                          decisions               quests

  Competence              Feeling capable of      Progress bars and
                          achieving goals         meaningful achievements

  Relatedness             Feeling connected to    Cooperative missions
                          other people            and social activities
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Scientific evidence supporting the design

Sailer et al. (2017) conducted an experimental study examining how
different gamification elements influence psychological needs.

The researchers found that avatars, meaningful stories, and teammates
positively influenced feelings of social relatedness. Badges,
leaderboards, and performance graphs were associated with
competence-related outcomes.

**Implication:** If the primary objective is improving social
connectedness, cooperative mechanics and meaningful interactions are
more directly aligned with the objective than a competitive leaderboard.

The experiment does not prove that RemoteConnect will reduce workplace
loneliness. That outcome still needs testing.

Reference: Sailer, M. et al. (2017), *How gamification motivates: An
experimental study of the effects of specific game design elements on
psychological need satisfaction*. [Google
Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=How+gamification+motivates+Sailer+2017)

A simple prototype could be built in Figma, Canva, or PowerPoint. It is

  Screen                  Function                Example
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  1\. Dashboard           Displays available      "Welcome back! Choose
                          activities              your weekly quest."

  2\. Quest Selection     Employees select their  Coffee Connection,
                          preferred challenges    Knowledge Hero, Helping
                                                  Hand

  3\. Quest Completion    Participants confirm    "Quest completed!"
                          completion              

  4\. Team Progress       Displays collective     "Your team has
                          achievements            completed 15/20
                                                  activities."
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Example gameplay

1.  An employee opens RemoteConnect.
2.  They select the quest "Knowledge Hero."
3.  They share a useful Excel shortcut with a colleague.
4.  After confirming the interaction, they receive a badge.
5.  The activity contributes to their team's collective progress.

### Why should this improve engagement?

The intended psychological mechanism is:

**Voluntary activities → More meaningful interactions → Stronger team
connection → Potentially higher engagement**

The badge is not the actual solution. It helps motivate or recognize the
behavior, while the meaningful social interaction is what may improve
connectedness.

## 8. Playtest and feedback

The assignment requires at least one real playtest.

A 15-minute session with four to six classmates would be suitable.

### Suggested test procedure

**Phase 1: Introduction (2 minutes)**

Explain the problem and how RemoteConnect works.

**Phase 2: Gameplay (8 minutes)**

Let participants select and complete activities. For example: - Find
someone who knows a useful productivity trick. - Share one skill that
could help someone else. - Work with a partner to complete a short
collaborative challenge.

**Phase 3: Feedback (5 minutes)**

Ask participants to rate:

  Question                                              Rating Scale
  ----------------------------------------------------- --------------
  Was the experience enjoyable?                         1--5
  Did the quests encourage interaction?                 1--5
  Did you feel connected to other participants?         1--5
  Would you voluntarily use this application?           1--5
  Did any game mechanic feel uncomfortable or forced?   Open answer

Record the actual results and describe what you changed in the
prototype.

For example, if participants dislike competitive rankings, you could
remove the leaderboard and emphasize shared progress instead. Only
report such a change if your playtest actually produces that feedback.

## 12. Final recommendation

Focus on **"Social Isolation and Reduced Knowledge Sharing in Remote
Work"**.

Three reasons support this choice:

1.  **Scientific support:** Research identifies fewer informal
    interactions and challenges involving loneliness and knowledge
    exchange in remote work environments.
2.  **Clear gamification purpose:** The solution encourages cooperation
    and social connection, rather than simply distributing points for
    completing ordinary work.
3.  **Practical prototype and testing:** Classmates can participate in
    cooperative quests, evaluate the experience, and provide measurable
    feedback.

The project should follow this coherent structure:

**Business Challenge → Scientific Evidence → Psychological Theory → Game
Mechanics → Prototype → Evaluation**

This provides a foundation for both the 15-page report and the 20-minute
presentation.
