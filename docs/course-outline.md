## Objective

In groups of 3–5, you will design and prototype a gamified business interaction or serious business game. The goal is to demonstrate your ability to apply theoretical concepts (MDA framework, motivation theory, ethics) to a real-world business context.

## Deliverables

### 1. Project Report (max. 15 pages)

- **Business Context & Challenge**
  - Describe the organization, industry, or function (HR, innovation, strategy, etc.).
  - Identify the business problem or opportunity (e.g., low engagement, innovation inertia, customer retention).
- **Objective**
  - Define the purpose of your gamification project.
  - What measurable change do you want to see?
- **Players**
  - Define target user groups (employees, managers, customers, stakeholders).
  - Include player types (Bartle/Marczewski) and motivational drivers.
- **Game Mechanics & Dynamics**
  - Justify your design choices with frameworks (MDA, Octalysis, Flow, Self-Determination Theory).
  - Explain why these mechanics fit the business objective and players.
- **Prototype**
  - Present your prototype (can be digital mock-ups, board game prototype, roleplay, or app wireframe).
  - Document the rules, interactions, and aesthetics.
- **Playtest & Feedback**
  - Conduct at least one playtest session (in class or outside).
  - Summarize feedback and what you changed as a result.
- **Ethics & Reflection**
  - Critically reflect on ethical aspects (motivation vs. manipulation, inclusivity, sustainability).

### 2. Presentation (20 minutes + 10 minutes Q&A)

- Walk the class through the design process.
- Show your prototype in action (demo or video).
- Highlight lessons learned.

## Grading Breakdown for Gamification Project (50%)

### A. Written Report – 30% of final grade (= 60% of project grade)

- **Business Context & Objective (5%)** – clarity of problem, relevance of challenge.
- **Theoretical Application (10%)** – use of frameworks (MDA, MVE, motivation theory, etc.).
- **Design Quality (5%)** – coherence between mechanics, dynamics, aesthetics, and objectives.
- **Prototype & Playtest Evidence (5%)** – clear documentation of prototype + lessons learned.
- **Reflection & Ethics (5%)** – critical awareness of manipulation, inclusivity, sustainability.

### B. Presentation – 20% of final grade (= 40% of project grade)

- **Clarity & Storytelling (5%)** – structured, engaging, easy to follow.
- **Demonstration of Prototype (5%)** – effective, concrete, vivid.
- **Connection to Theory (5%)** – showing why design choices make sense.
- **Q&A Performance (5%)** – ability to respond to questions, show mastery.

## Reflective Essays – 50% of Final Grade

In addition to the group project, each student will write two individual reflective essays over the course of the semester. These essays are designed to deepen personal understanding and to link theoretical insights to real-world gamification phenomena.

### Objectives

- Demonstrate personal and dialectical reflection on the weekly topics discussed in class.
- Apply relevant theories and frameworks (e.g., Flow, SDT, Fogg Model, HEXAD, etc.) to interpret or critique real-world examples.
- Strengthen academic writing and referencing skills.

- **Format:** academic essay with introduction, argumentation, and conclusion.
- **References:** include at least three academic references per essay.
  - All references must be verifiable (APA style, in-text citations + reference list).

- Depth of reflection and originality of thought.
- Integration of theory and literature.
- Clarity, coherence, and structure.
- Proper use of sources and academic integrity.
